# Keep Haiti incident evidence private

Status: accepted and implemented for local review on 20 October 2026.

The investigation's contributors require anonymity because attribution could endanger their work or staff. Preserve original evidence for editorial checking, but omit provider identities, checking notes, source-linked identifiers, and links to the working evidence workbook from public feeds, browser payloads, generated public caches, and interface controls. Hiding a source button alone does not meet this requirement. Public methodology should use editor-approved wording; attribution for public geographic reference data remains separate and should be retained as required.

The `haiti-display-v1` schema explicitly projects display fields at the live-feed, snapshot and download boundaries. The restricted editorial archive was verified against the original workbook before changing Sheet1. Private backup tabs were removed from the public workbook after verification; Sheet1 and its existing published URL were retained. Original casualty data, evidence notes and the source-to-public-ID mapping are stored outside this repository. This change does not erase historical access or Google revision history.

The testing edition initially contained only the supplied 2025 incidents, including the report with an unconfirmed date. On 11 November 2026, the review selection was extended with provisional 2026 entries after excluding red-marked source rows. The original 2025 selection is preserved. Entries awaiting a narrative remain explicitly pending rather than receiving invented incident descriptions. Local, staging, build caches and CSV downloads all use that selection. The broader live feed is not fetched, preventing unselected records or context events from returning during a rebuild. Original source workbooks and private backups remain unchanged. Reimporting source changes requires an explicit editorial review.

On 25 November 2026, the blue-marked 2025 editorial updates added six records and revised five existing records. Two Fontaine reports replace the previous combined report, preserving their existing public identities. The final source also confirms the formerly pending date as 9 March 2025. An older report absent from the latest source remains included until its omission is confirmed. All 263 selected 2026 reports remain unchanged. The revised snapshot contains 196 reports from 2025 and 459 overall. The source workbook backup, full English translation drafts and row reconciliation remain outside the repository; only display-safe adaptations enter the preview.
