# TNH Storytelling

Editorial vocabulary for the investigations presented on this platform.

## Haiti drone investigation

**Incident**:
A distinct reported occurrence involving explosive drones. The existence of a separate source row or identifier does not by itself establish that two reports describe different incidents.
_Avoid_: Drone, aircraft, strike as interchangeable names for an incident.

**Recorded drone count**:
The number supplied for an incident in the investigation's data. This does not establish the number of unique aircraft, and an unknown count is different from a confirmed zero.

**Known drone subtotal**:
The sum of the numeric recorded drone counts within a stated set of incidents. Incidents with unknown counts remain part of the investigation without contributing an invented number.

**Incident location**:
The reported point associated with an incident, which may represent a locality rather than an exact attack site. Decimal precision does not establish positional accuracy.

**Source narrative**:
The original editorial text supplied for an incident. It remains the record of that wording when a translation or public display narrative is prepared.

**Display narrative**:
The text selected for the public interactive. It follows the investigation's publication decisions, including the exclusion of casualty figures, while the source narrative remains intact.

**Private evidence**:
Source identities, verification remarks, source-linked identifiers, and other material retained for editorial checking rather than public display or distribution.

**Context event**:
An editorially selected event that explains the investigation's chronology. It is separate from an incident and contributes no drones to a tally.

**Local review set**:
The material made available for reviewing the interactive before its final editorial selection. Inclusion does not establish approval for publication.

**Publication set**:
The explicit selection of incidents and context events approved for a particular public edition of the investigation.
